Hybrid Structure — Main Lift + Bodybuilding Volume
One main lift per day, always the same one: Squat (Mon), Bench (Tue), Deadlift (Wed), Overhead Press (Thu). Only the top set follows the phase progression down toward a 1-2RM by Week 16 — this is your strength thread through the week.

Everything else is bodybuilding volume: 3-4 exercises per muscle group, 10-18 rep range, shorter rest, multiple angles per body part (e.g. Cable + DB lateral raise, standing + seated calf raise). This is what builds size and shape — it doesn't need to peak for a max, so it stays high volume the whole 16 weeks.

Only one vertical press day: Overhead Press appears only on Thursday now — Tuesday's bench day stays horizontal-press focused (bench, incline DB) so your shoulders aren't under barbell pressing load twice in the same week.
Core, Lower Back & Calves
Mon: Weighted Plank (anti-extension) + Back Extension (erectors) + Standing Calf Raise (gastrocnemius)
Tue: Hanging Leg Raise (lower abs) + Pallof Press (anti-rotation)
Wed: Cable Woodchopper (obliques) + Seated Calf Raise (soleus) — deadlift itself is the erector work
Thu: Cable Crunch (upper abs) + Bird Dog (spinal stability)

Standing and seated calf raises hit different heads of the calf — standing loads the gastrocnemius, seated loads the soleus. Core angles rotate daily — anti-extension, anti-rotation, flexion, stability — training it the way it actually works under a barbell rather than just crunches every session.
Body Recomposition Focus
Build wider: Overhead Press + lateral raises (shoulders), pull-ups (lats)

Define narrower: Hip abduction, hip thrust, core anti-rotation

Reduce cortisol: 4 strength days, 1 conditioning day

Friday — LA Fitness Spin Class: Zone 2-3 effort, not max intensity. The one session specifically targeting midsection fat without adding to weekly cortisol load.
